# Quiet Please! Privacy Policy

*Last updated: [November 1st, 2026]*

Quiet Please! ("we", "the app") helps you find and rank quiet cafes and share them with friends. This policy explains what we collect, how it's used, who can see it, and your choices. Contact: **quietpleaseadmin@gmail.com**.

## 1. What we collect

- **Account details:** your name, username, email address, and password. The password is handled by Firebase Authentication, and we can't read it.
- **Profile details:** your home city, cafe goal, and a profile picture if you add one.
- **Content you create:** cafe rankings and scores, notes, favorite dishes, cafe details you report (WiFi, outlets, noise level, and so on), bookmarks and bookmark notes, photos you upload, and the people you follow.
- **Activity:** lines in your friends' feed such as "ranked", "bookmarked," or achievements, and your challenge progress.
- **Location:** your approximate or precise location, only while you use the app and only if you allow it. It's used to find and sort cafes near you.
- **Safety data:** reports you file, and the list of people you block.

We don't collect your contacts, advertising identifiers, or payment information, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

## 2. How we use it

We use your information to run your account, show cafes near you, build your rankings and your friends' feed, review photos and reports, keep the community safe, and answer support requests. We don't sell your data.

## 3. Who can see what

- **Private to you:** your email, bookmarks and bookmark notes, and your list of blocked people.
- **Visible to other signed-in members:** your name or username, profile picture, rankings, notes, favorite dishes, activity, and approved photos.
- **Photos:** every photo is scanned automatically and reviewed by our team before anyone else can see it. Explicit photos are declined automatically and deleted.
- **Reports:** A report is seen only by our team. The person you report isn't told who reported them.

## 4. Services we use

- **Google Firebase** (Authentication, Firestore database, Storage and Cloud Functions) stores your account and content.
- **Google Places** supplies cafe information. Your search text and the location you search around are sent to Google through our server so we can get results.
- **Google Cloud Vision** scans uploaded photos for explicit content.

These providers process data for us under their own terms and privacy policies.

## 5. Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete your account in the app under Settings, then Delete account. This removes your profile, username, rankings and bookmarks. You can also email us to ask for a copy of your data or to have it deleted.

## 6. Your choices

You can turn location off in iOS Settings, change or remove your profile details, delete your rankings and bookmarks, block or report anyone, and delete your account.

## 7. Children

Quiet Please! isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us data, email us and we'll delete it.

## 8. Security

We send data over encrypted connections and protect it with access rules. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Changes

If we make important changes, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app.
